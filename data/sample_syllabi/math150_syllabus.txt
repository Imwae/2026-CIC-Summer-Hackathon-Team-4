COURSE DESCRIPTION

This course covers the fundamentals of single-variable calculus including limits,
continuity, differentiation, and integration. Topics include techniques of
differentiation (product, quotient, and chain rules), applications of
derivatives (related rates, optimization, curve sketching), the definite
integral, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, and basic integration techniques.

REQUIRED TEXTBOOK

Stewart, J. (2020). Calculus: Early Transcendentals, 9th ed. Cengage Learning.
(Chapters 1–6 are covered in this course.)

ASSIGNMENT DETAILS

Weekly Quizzes (Weeks 2–14, 15%)
Short 15-minute quizzes administered at the start of Thursday tutorials covering
material from the previous week's lectures. The lowest two quiz scores are
dropped. Each quiz tests core computational skills (limits, derivatives,
integrals). Estimated preparation: 1–2 hours per week of practice problems.

Problem Set 1 — Limits and Differentiation (Week 5, 10%)
A set of 8–10 multi-part problems covering epsilon-delta proofs of limits,
limit evaluation techniques, and basic differentiation rules (power, product,
quotient, and chain rules). Estimated preparation: 2 weeks of steady work,
approximately 12 hours total.

MIDTERM EXAM (2025-10-27, 25%)
Covers Weeks 1–7: limits, continuity, definition of the derivative,
differentiation techniques, and applications of derivatives including related
rates and linear approximation. The exam is closed-book, 90 minutes, held in
class during the regular lecture slot. Budget 2 weeks of review, approximately
18 hours of study.

Problem Set 2 — Integration Techniques (Week 11, 10%)
A set of 8–10 multi-part problems covering Riemann sums, the Fundamental
Theorem of Calculus, substitution, and integration by parts. Estimated
preparation: 2 weeks of effort, approximately 14 hours total.

FINAL EXAM (2025-12-16, 40%)
Comprehensive, covering all course material from Weeks 1–13 including limits,
differentiation, applications of derivatives, and integration. Three-hour
sit-down exam scheduled during the final exam period in the PAC gymnasium.
Start reviewing at least 3 weeks in advance; expect 25–30 hours of study time.
