COURSE DESCRIPTION

This course introduces the fundamental concepts of computer science including
problem-solving techniques, algorithm design, data representation, and
introductory programming in Python. Students will develop computational thinking
skills applicable to diverse disciplines.

REQUIRED TEXTBOOK

Downey, A. (2015). Think Python: How to Think Like a Computer Scientist, 2nd ed.
O'Reilly Media. (Available free online.)

ASSIGNMENT DETAILS

Assignment 1 — Variables and Control Flow (Week 4, 10%)
Write programs that demonstrate variable assignment, conditional logic, and
loops. Estimated preparation: 2 weeks of steady work, approximately 10 hours
total.

Assignment 2 — Functions and Modularity (due 2025-10-20, 15%)
Design and implement modular solutions using functions, parameters, and return
values. Expect roughly 14 hours of effort spread over 2 weeks.

Assignment 3 — Data Structures (due 2025-11-17, 15%)
Implement solutions using lists, dictionaries, and basic file I/O. Plan for
about 16 hours of work over 3 weeks.

MIDTERM EXAM (Week 7, 25%)
Covers lectures 1 through 18 (Weeks 1–6). The exam is closed-book, 90 minutes,
held in class. Budget 2 weeks of review, approximately 15 hours of study.

FINAL EXAM (2025-12-12, 35%)
Comprehensive, covering the entire course. Three-hour sit-down exam scheduled
during the final exam period. Start reviewing at least 3 weeks in advance;
expect 20–25 hours of study time.
